# MAP — read-only level survey

Type `MAP` during play or press the toolbar Map button (also available while paused). The entire CURRENT level fits on one screen, including concealed replacement terrain, undiscovered items, offscreen enemies and active passages. Pink outlines identify hidden blocks; cyan diamonds are passages; a red/white dot marks the player and a rectangle the current camera. Front covers are omitted so hidden content is visible. Separate bonus worlds remain separate levels, each with its own map.

The survey reads a copy of the live map, applies active blocks' replacement IDs to that copy, and renders original indexed assets to its own canvas. It never calls World.step or alters discovery, timers, health, camera, RNG or persistence. Empty black padding is cropped for narrow stages, without hiding authored visible terrain/entities. Any fresh key closes it and is consumed through keyup, including repeated Space/arrow/function keys. A tap or Back button also closes it. Previous play/pause state and graphics mode are retained. Music selection is retained; the game simulation is paused. M alone still toggles sound after a short MAP-prefix recognition delay; F3 and the sound button remain immediate.

Implementation: `dist/js/level-map.js`, `app.js`, `cheats.js`; unit and browser checks in `tests/level-map.test.mjs`, `tests/cheats.test.mjs`, `scripts/verify-level-map-browser.mjs`. `review/` contains all-stage checks and desktop/fullscreen example maps. This is a browser-only inspection feature, not a recovered DOS cheat. No original executable is run.
