Earthy, warm, intimate live acoustic ensemble. Natural adult male lead singing clearly in Slovak, with a small mixed group joining the refrain in simple close harmony. Animated, hearty and melodic, with sincere folk delivery and a little adventure-game humor. No imitation of a specific singer, no pop/rock backing, no drum kit, no electronic instruments, no cinematic orchestra, no English words, no spoken introduction. Begin singing quickly so the short sample contains one verse and the refrain. Sing the following original Slovak lyrics in order; labels are not sung.

VERSE:
Pod horou sa oheň páli,
hladný junák skáče skaly.
V jednej ruke kyjak nesie,
večeru si hľadá v lese.

REFRAIN:
Hej, cez jamu, ponad skaly,
medvede nás nedohnali!
